### Notable Implementation Detail: measure_text_cached Uses peek+clone

Plan 01 spec called for pop-on-hit semantics. The final implementation (after Plan 04 debugging, commit b454878) uses peek() + clone():

- glyph_cache.peek(&key) leaves the entry in cache on hit
- Buffer is cloned out; original stays in LRU
- return_buffers_to_cache() refreshes LRU recency

This is strictly better: duplicate lines (same text multiple times per frame) hit cache on every occurrence rather than missing after the first pop. This is why hit rate reached 98% instead of the 54% observed with pop semantics.
