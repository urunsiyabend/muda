## Decisions Made

- **Pop from LRU on cache hit, not peek:** glyphon Buffer is not Clone-without-cost; the Buffer must be owned by PaintCommand::Text for the renderer. Popping (removing from cache) gives ownership cleanly. After render, the buffer is returned.
- **drain_buffers_for_cache() replaces clear():** The old clear() dropped all pending Buffers. The new drain variant extracts them first, collects those with a cache_key, and returns them to the LRU cache. This is the heart of the reuse loop.
- **cache_key: None for existing call sites:** All pre-existing paint_text() calls pass None, meaning their buffers are not cached. Cache opt-in is per-call-site, enabling incremental migration.
- **AtomicBool for CLI flags:** parse_perf_flags() called once at startup; flags are stored in statics read per-frame with Ordering::Relaxed. No overhead vs. the pre-existing SHOW_FPS pattern.
